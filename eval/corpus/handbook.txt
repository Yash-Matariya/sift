ACME CORP EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK
Revised February 2026. Supersedes all previous versions.


1. REMOTE WORK — ELIGIBILITY

All full-time employees who have completed their probation period of three
months are eligible to work remotely. Contractors are not eligible under this
policy and should refer to their individual agreements. Employees on a formal
performance improvement plan are required to work from an office for the
duration of that plan.

Eligibility is not transferable between teams. An employee moving to a new team
retains remote eligibility but must agree core hours afresh with the new
manager within two weeks of the move.


2. REMOTE WORK — CORE HOURS

Remote employees must be reachable between 11:00 and 16:00 in the timezone of
their assigned team. Outside core hours, employees may arrange their schedule
as they see fit, provided commitments to colleagues and customers are met.

Meetings should not be scheduled outside core hours without the agreement of
everyone invited. Recurring meetings outside core hours require director
approval and must be reviewed every quarter.


3. REMOTE WORK — EQUIPMENT

The company provides a laptop and one external monitor to every employee. A
docking station is available on request. Employees may claim up to 300 GBP per
year towards a desk or chair. Claims require a receipt and manager approval
before purchase, not after. Approval obtained after a purchase will not be
honoured.

Equipment remains company property and must be returned within ten working days
of the last day of employment. The desk and chair allowance is an exception:
those items belong to the employee once the claim is settled.


4. REMOTE WORK — OFFICE ATTENDANCE

Employees living more than 80 kilometres from the nearest office are not
required to attend in person. Those within 80 kilometres are expected in the
office two days per month, and travel for these days is not reimbursed.

Team offsites are separate from the monthly attendance expectation. Travel and
accommodation for an offsite are arranged and paid for by the company, and
attendance at offsites is expected regardless of distance.


5. REMOTE WORK — SECURITY

Remote work must not be conducted over public wifi without the company VPN.
Devices must have full-disk encryption enabled. Screens must be locked when
unattended, including at home.

Company data must not be copied to personal devices or personal cloud storage
under any circumstances. Suspected device loss or theft must be reported to
the security team within four hours, at any hour of the day.


6. EXPENSES — SUBMISSION

Expenses must be submitted within sixty days of the date on the receipt.
Submissions after sixty days require a written explanation and are approved at
the discretion of the finance team. Expenses over 500 GBP require approval
before the money is spent.

Receipts must be legible and show the date, the vendor and the amount. A card
statement is not a receipt. Expenses submitted without a receipt are rejected
regardless of amount.


7. EXPENSES — TRAVEL

Standard class rail travel is reimbursed in full. First class is reimbursed
only where it was cheaper than standard at the time of booking, and the
comparison must be attached to the claim. Flights of under four hours are
booked in economy.

Mileage in a personal vehicle is reimbursed at 45 pence per mile for the first
ten thousand miles in a tax year, and 25 pence per mile thereafter. Parking and
tolls are reimbursed; speeding and parking fines are not.


8. EXPENSES — MEALS

Where an employee is required to stay away overnight, meals are reimbursed up
to 40 GBP per day. Alcohol is not reimbursed. Client entertainment is handled
separately and requires prior director approval.

Meals during a normal working day at a normal place of work are not reimbursed,
whether the employee is at home or in an office.


9. LEAVE — ANNUAL LEAVE

Full-time employees receive twenty-eight days of annual leave per year,
inclusive of public holidays. Leave accrues monthly from the start date. Up to
five unused days may be carried into the following year and must be taken by
31 March or they are lost.

Leave requests of five consecutive days or fewer require manager approval.
Longer requests require manager and director approval, and should be submitted
at least four weeks in advance.


10. LEAVE — SICK LEAVE

Sick leave is not deducted from annual leave. Absences of up to seven
consecutive days may be self-certified. Beyond seven days a fit note from a
doctor is required and must be sent to the people team, not to the line
manager.

Employees are asked to notify their manager before 10:00 on the first day of
absence, by message or call. There is no expectation of a reason beyond
"unwell".


11. LEAVE — PARENTAL LEAVE

Primary caregivers receive twenty-six weeks of leave at full pay, followed by
thirteen weeks at statutory pay. Secondary caregivers receive eight weeks at
full pay. Both must have twelve months of continuous service at the expected
date of birth or placement.

Parental leave may be taken in up to three separate blocks within the first
eighteen months, by agreement with the manager.


12. WORKING PRACTICES — CODE REVIEW

Every change to production code is reviewed by at least one other engineer
before it is merged. Reviews are expected within one working day. A review is
a conversation, not a gate: an approving review with comments is normal, and
the author decides which suggestions to act on unless a comment is marked as
blocking.

Changes larger than four hundred lines should be split where possible. Reviewers
are entitled to ask for a large change to be broken up before reviewing it.


13. WORKING PRACTICES — ON CALL

On-call rotations run for one week, beginning Wednesday at 10:00. No engineer
is placed on call within their first three months. Compensation is 200 GBP per
week on call, plus 75 GBP for any night in which an incident page is received.

Time worked overnight during an incident is taken back as time off in lieu
within the following two weeks, arranged with the manager.


14. WORKING PRACTICES — INCIDENTS

Every incident of severity two or above gets a written review within five
working days. Reviews are blameless: they describe what the system allowed to
happen, not who typed the command. Naming individuals as causes is not
permitted in a review document.

Action items from a review are assigned an owner and a date, and are tracked to
completion in the same way as any other work.


15. EQUIPMENT AND ACCOUNTS — JOINING

New joiners receive their laptop on or before their first day. Accounts are
provisioned by the end of the first morning. A buddy is assigned for the first
month and is expected to make contact before the start date.

Anyone who has not received working accounts by the end of their first day
should contact the people team directly rather than waiting.
